## Desktop assessment

The search field is now the dominant discovery action, while the mega menu carries deeper category/room/design-service navigation. The luxury editorial hierarchy remains intact and commercial density increases only where it aids comparison.

## Tablet assessment

The 1024–820px experience now uses a dedicated search row and compact header rather than a squeezed desktop nav. PLP density drops cleanly, filters move into sheets, and the PDP exposes product identity and price before the main gallery dominates the screen.

## Mobile assessment

The mobile layout retains the five-item bottom navigation while the top header is simplified to Menu / Brand / Bag plus a dedicated search row. Product cards use a two-column marketplace grid, internal horizontal rails are contained, and PDP purchase actions remain fixed above the bottom navigation.

## Sacred Luxury

Sacred Spaces retains its bespoke ThemeOS look. The audit only adjusts responsive hierarchy and interaction density; it does not flatten the collection into the generic Marketplace Luxe visual treatment.
